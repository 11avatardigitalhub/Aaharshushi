# 🌿 Aahar Shudhi - Lead Management System

## 📋 Overview

Enterprise-grade Lead Management System for Ayurvedic Product Company - **Aahar Shudhi**. Designed for tele-calling teams with complete lead lifecycle management.

## 🚀 Features

### Core Features
- ✅ Lead Capture & Management
- ✅ Kanban Board (Drag & Drop)
- ✅ Status Tracking
- ✅ Call Notes & Remarks
- ✅ Follow-up & Reminders
- ✅ Real-time Sync
- ✅ Dashboard & Analytics
- ✅ Advanced Filters

### Quick Actions
- ✅ One-click Call
- ✅ One-click WhatsApp
- ✅ Quick Notes
- ✅ Schedule Follow-up

### Multi-Tenant
- ✅ Role-based Access (Admin, Team Lead, Agent)
- ✅ Team Management
- ✅ Region Management
- ✅ Data Isolation
